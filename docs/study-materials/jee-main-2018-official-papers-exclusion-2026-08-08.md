# JEE Main 2018 official-paper exclusion

No JEE Main 2018 B.E./B.Tech. Paper 1 PDF is included in the production
seed.

CBSE administered JEE Main in 2018. Its permanent press archive and preserved
official-host material were checked on 8 August 2026:

- [CBSE 2018 press archive](https://www.cbse.gov.in/cbsenew/press_archive.html)
- [Offline-exam conduct release](https://www.cbse.gov.in/cbsenew/Press_Notes/2018/16.%20Press%20Release-%20JEE%20%28Main%29%20sucessfully%20conducted.pdf)
- [Online-exam conduct release](https://www.cbse.gov.in/cbsenew/Press_Notes/2018/20.%20Press%20Note-%20CBSE%20Successfully%20conducts%20online%20Edition%20of%20JEE%20%28Main%29%202018.pdf)
- [Paper 1 result release](https://www.cbse.gov.in/cbsenew/Press_Notes/2018/21.%20JEE%20Result%202018.pdf)
- [Preserved official information bulletin](https://web.archive.org/web/20180823205033id_/https://jeemain.nic.in/WebInfo/Handler/FileHandler.ashx?i=File&ii=88&iii=Y)

The official bulletin schedules Paper 1 offline on 8 April 2018 and computer-
based examinations on 15 and 16 April, with a second shift where required.
It also states that the offline answer keys and OMR images, and the computer-
based answer keys, would be displayed only from 24 to 27 April 2018. CBSE's
conduct and result releases confirm those examination dates.

A [preserved 30 April 2018 snapshot of the official JEE Main homepage](https://web.archive.org/web/20180430165923id_/https://jeemain.nic.in/webinfo/Public/Home.aspx)
links to `QuestionPapers2018.htm`. That question-paper page was not captured, and no
complete official Paper 1 PDFs behind it were found in the permanent CBSE
archive or the preserved PDF inventory of the official JEE Main host. The only
preserved official-host PDFs found by the audit were the information bulletin
and a bilingual e-admit-card notice; both were downloaded, rendered and
visually checked. The three relevant live CBSE releases and the bulletin pages
covering dates, modes, shifts and the response-display window were also
rendered and visually checked.

No coaching-site scan, solved paper, memory-based reconstruction or candidate
response sheet was substituted. A future production package should be
prepared only after complete official copies become available or the user
supplies redistribution-approved official originals.
