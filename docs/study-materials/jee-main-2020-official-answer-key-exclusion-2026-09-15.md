# JEE Main 2020 official answer-key exclusion

No JEE Main 2020 Paper 1 answer key or worked solution is included in the
production seed.

NTA administered JEE Main in 2020. Its current official archive and result
records were checked on 15 September 2026 for both the January and the
April/September sessions.

The official [NTA notice archive](https://www.nta.ac.in/NoticeBoardArchive)
retains the Paper 1 result releases for January and September 2020, but does
not expose a complete public Paper 1 final answer-key document. The current
[JEE Main archive](https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/document-category/archive/page/11/)
does expose an explicitly labelled April/September 2020 answer-key document
for Paper 2 B.Arch/B.Planning. It does not list an equivalent Paper 1 final
key. Paper 2 is excluded from this JEE engineering catalogue.

An official NTA [RTI response](https://nta.ac.in/Download/RTI/RTISM_20220303121530.pdf)
confirms that a candidate's January 2020 final answer key existed in the
assessment process, but it concerns an individual request and does not publish
a complete official public Paper 1 key or redistribution permission.

The existing official-paper audit independently found that the NTA archive
retained candidate-response access but no stable, complete direct English
Paper 1 PDFs for the six January and ten September shifts. No coaching-site
key, reconstructed answer table, candidate response record, question paper or
third-party worked solution was substituted.

A future answer-key package may be prepared only if NTA publishes a complete
official public Paper 1 key, or when the owner supplies an official original
with permission that clearly covers redistribution on JEENEETARD.
