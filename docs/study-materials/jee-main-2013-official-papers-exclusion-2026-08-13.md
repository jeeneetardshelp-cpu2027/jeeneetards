# JEE Main 2013 official-paper exclusion

No JEE Main 2013 B.E./B.Tech. Paper 1 PDF is included in the production
seed.

The first JEE Main was administered by CBSE. Its official records establish
five distinct Paper 1 examination dates:

- 7 April 2013 - offline, pen-and-paper mode
- 9 April 2013 - online, computer-based mode
- 22 April 2013 - online, computer-based mode
- 23 April 2013 - online, computer-based mode
- 25 April 2013 - online, computer-based mode

The [archived official CBSE public notice](https://web.archive.org/web/20130405000000id_/https://www.cbse.gov.in/pub%20not%201_jee13_2013.pdf)
states that different question-paper sets were used on those dates and that
each set covered Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry. The notice was recovered
from an Internet Archive capture of the original CBSE URL, downloaded and
visually checked in full.

The [CBSE Annual Report 2013-14](https://www.cbse.gov.in/cbsenew/annual-report/CBSE_Annual_Report_2013-14.pdf)
independently confirms that Paper 1 was offered in offline and online modes,
records the same five dates, and distinguishes Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech.) from
Paper 2 (B.Arch./B.Planning). Its relevant JEE Main pages 30-31 were rendered
and visually checked.

The original `jeemain.nic.in` question-paper page and PDF inventory were then
checked through the Internet Archive. Available official-host question-paper
captures begin with later examination years; the archived inventory did not
expose stable, complete Paper 1 PDFs for any of the five 2013 dates. The live
CBSE site retains the annual report and indexed historical exam evidence, but
the old notice URL is no longer directly available and no complete 2013 Paper
1 PDF set is exposed.

Third-party sites list reconstructed papers, coaching solutions and memory-
based compilations for 2013. None was substituted because JEENEETARD's policy
for exam papers is official-source-only.

A future production package may be prepared only when all five complete
official Paper 1 sets become available from CBSE, the original JEE Main host,
or when the owner supplies originals with explicit redistribution permission.
Each accepted PDF must be source-linked, hashed, page-counted and visually
checked in full before seeding.

Production was checked read-only on 13 August 2026. It contained two 2013
previous-year papers, both correctly identified as JEE Advanced Paper 1 and
Paper 2, and no JEE Main 2013 Paper 1 record.
