# JEE Main 2018 official answer-key exclusion

No JEE Main 2018 Paper 1 answer key or worked solution is included in the
production seed.

CBSE administered JEE Main in 2018. The official information bulletin,
preserved JEE Main host, permanent CBSE press archive and the official-host PDF
inventory were checked on 15 September 2026.

The [preserved official information bulletin](https://web.archive.org/web/20180823205033id_/https://jeemain.nic.in/WebInfo/Handler/FileHandler.ashx?i=File&ii=88&iii=Y)
schedules Paper 1 on 8 April in pen-and-paper mode and on 15 and 16 April in
computer-based mode. It says that the offline answer keys and OMR images, and
the computer-based answer keys, would be displayed from 24 to 27 April 2018
for the challenge process. The [official 30 April homepage snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20180430165923id_/https://jeemain.nic.in/webinfo/Public/Home.aspx)
instead links to the question-paper page; it does not provide a stable public
answer-key PDF.

The archived official JEE Main PDF inventory was checked, including the
preserved official [e-Admit Card notice](https://web.archive.org/web/20180430144139id_/https://jeemain.nic.in/WebInfo/Handler/FileHandler.ashx?i=File&ii=93&iii=Y).
That two-page notice is dated 9 March 2018 and only describes Paper 1 dates
and e-Admit Cards. No complete public Paper 1 answer-key PDF appears in the
official inventory.

No coaching-site key, reconstructed answer table, candidate response record,
question paper, Paper 2 B.Arch/B.Planning key or third-party worked solution
was substituted. A future answer-key package may be prepared only when CBSE,
NTA or another official government source provides a complete public Paper 1
key, or when the owner supplies an official original with permission that
clearly covers redistribution on JEENEETARD.

The separate 2018 question-paper exclusion remains unchanged: the complete
official Paper 1 set is unavailable, so no answer key is attached to an
incomplete or unofficial paper record.
