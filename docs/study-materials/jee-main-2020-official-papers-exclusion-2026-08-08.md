# JEE Main 2020 official-paper exclusion

No JEE Main 2020 B.E./B.Tech. Paper 1 PDF is included in the production seed.

The permanent NTA archive was checked on 8 August 2026 for both 2020
sessions. The official result releases confirm that Paper 1 was conducted in
six January shifts and ten September shifts:

- [January 2020 result release](https://www.nta.ac.in/Download/Notice/Notice_20200117173708.pdf)
- [September 2020 result release](https://nta.ac.in/Download/Notice/Notice_20200912035511.pdf)

The January release states that each candidate's question paper and recorded
responses were displayed online for verification. Those time-limited response
pages and the final answer-key/result material are represented in the
[NTA notice archive](https://www.nta.ac.in/NoticeBoardArchive), but no stable,
complete, direct official English Paper 1 PDFs for the sixteen shifts were
found.

Twenty-three live official NTA `ExamPaper` candidates from the 2020 archive
were downloaded and inspected before making this exclusion:

| Official candidate batch | Files checked | Verification result |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `Paper_20201106*.pdf` | 20 | Excluded: NEET `KANHA` test booklets with 180 questions and 720 maximum marks, in English and regional-language variants. |
| `Paper_20201202092744.pdf` | 1 | Excluded: UGC-NET Management paper. |
| `Paper_20201202094842.pdf` and `Paper_20201202094924.pdf` | 2 | Excluded: UGC-NET Commerce papers. |

No coaching-site reconstruction, memory-based paper or candidate response
sheet was substituted. A future production package should be prepared only
after complete official copies become available or the user supplies
redistribution-approved originals.
