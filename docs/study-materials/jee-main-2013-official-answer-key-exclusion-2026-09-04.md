# JEE Main 2013 official answer-key exclusion

No JEE Main 2013 Paper 1 answer key or worked solution is included in the
production seed.

CBSE administered the first JEE Main in 2013. The preserved official
`jeemain.nic.in` site, its complete public PDF inventory for the 2013 exam,
and the relevant official notices were checked on 4 September 2026.

The [official 29 April 2013 notice](https://web.archive.org/web/20130512123528id_/http://jeemain.nic.in:80/jeemain2013/pdf/public_notice_jee_main_29_04_2013.pdf)
allowed candidates to view their recorded offline OMR responses or online
question paper and responses from 30 April through 2 May. It did not publish
a general answer-key PDF.

The [official 13 May 2013 notice](https://web.archive.org/web/20130612054843id_/http://jeemain.nic.in:80/jeemain2013/pdf/publicNotice_1405.pdf)
then offered a photocopy of an individual candidate's OMR sheet, answer key
and calculation sheet for a fee, by request before 15 June. The notice says
the documents would be provided only to the candidate by speed post. It also
expressly says they would not be provided to an institution or school for
display, commercial purpose or print media.

The official-host PDF inventory contains the notices and administrative
records but no standalone public JEE Main 2013 Paper 1 answer-key PDF. This is
consistent with the candidate-specific access described by the notices.

Third-party coaching keys, reconstructed papers, memory-based answers and
candidate photocopies or scans were not substituted. They do not satisfy the
official-only requirement, and the official notice does not establish public
redistribution permission. A future package may be prepared only if CBSE/NTA
publishes a complete official public copy or the owner supplies an official
original together with permission that clearly covers redistribution on
JEENEETARD.

The separate 2013 question-paper exclusion remains unchanged: the complete
official five-date Paper 1 set could not be recovered, so no answer key is
attached to an incomplete or unofficial paper record.
