# JEE Main 2019 official-paper exclusion

No JEE Main 2019 B.E./B.Tech. Paper 1 PDF is included in the production seed.

The permanent NTA archive was checked on 8 August 2026 for both 2019
sessions. The official result releases confirm eight January Paper 1 shifts
and eight April Paper 1 shifts:

- [January 2019 result release](https://nta.ac.in/Download/Notice/PublicNoticeJEE%28Main%29January-2019Results.pdf)
- [April 2019 result release](https://nta.ac.in/Download/Notice/20190429155134.pdf)

The January release states that each candidate's question paper and recorded
responses were displayed online for verification. The April release says
candidates could see their responses to the question papers and challenge the
answer keys from 14 to 16 April 2019. Those time-limited candidate views and
the final answer keys/results are represented in the
[NTA notice archive](https://www.nta.ac.in/NoticeBoardArchive), but no stable,
complete, direct official English Paper 1 PDFs for the sixteen shifts were
found.

Both official result releases were downloaded, rendered in full and visually
checked. Searches of the official NTA `ExamPaper` host and permanent archive
did not expose a qualifying 2019 Paper 1 PDF.

No coaching-site reconstruction, memory-based paper or candidate response
sheet was substituted. A future production package should be prepared only
after complete official copies become available or the user supplies
redistribution-approved originals.
