# JEE Main 2016 official answer-key exclusion

No JEE Main 2016 Paper 1 answer key or worked solution is included in the
production seed.

CBSE administered JEE Main in 2016. The preserved official `jeemain.nic.in`
site and its PDF inventory were checked on 4 September 2026:

- [Official homepage during the answer-key window](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422200645id_/http://jeemain.nic.in:80/webinfo/public/home.aspx)
- [Official answer-key challenge login](https://web.archive.org/web/20160421151220id_/http://jeemain.nic.in:80/jeemainapp/KeyChallange/LoginKeyChallange.aspx)
- [Official homepage after the challenge window](https://web.archive.org/web/20160430030607id_/http://jeemain.nic.in:80/webinfo/Public/Home.aspx)
- [Official 27 April OMR-sheet photocopy notice](https://web.archive.org/web/20160501164100id_/http://jeemain.nic.in:80/WebInfo/Handler/FileHandler.ashx?i=File&ii=51&iii=Y)

The official homepage snapshots from 18 through 22 April 2016 link to
`View/Challenge - Answer Key of Online/Offline examination`. That link opens a
candidate login rather than a stable public answer-key PDF. Snapshots from 24
through 30 April do not retain a public answer-key link. The official-host PDF
inventory contains no 2016 Paper 1 answer-key PDF. Its only late-April document
is a public notice offering candidates a paid photocopy of their OMR and
calculation sheets; it is not an answer key.

The six official 2016 Paper 1 question papers already catalogued by JEENEETARD
remain valid, but none includes answers or worked solutions. No coaching-site
key, memory-based reconstruction, candidate response sheet or unofficial scan
was substituted. A future answer-key package should be prepared only if a
complete official copy becomes available or the user supplies an official
original they are permitted to redistribute.
