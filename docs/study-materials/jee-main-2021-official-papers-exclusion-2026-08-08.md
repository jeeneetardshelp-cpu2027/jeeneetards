# JEE Main 2021 official-paper exclusion

No JEE Main 2021 B.E./B.Tech. Paper 1 PDF is included in the production seed.

The permanent NTA archive was checked on 8 August 2026 for all four 2021
sessions. Its official notices confirm that question papers with each
candidate's recorded responses were made available through the JEE Main
candidate challenge flow:

- [February session notice](https://nta.ac.in/Download/Notice/Notice_20210302141413.pdf)
- [March session notice](https://nta.ac.in/Download/Notice/Notice_20210320162235.pdf)
- [July session notice](https://nta.ac.in/Download/Notice/Notice_20210729150454.pdf)
- [August/September session notice](https://www.nta.ac.in/Download/Notice/Notice_20210906192355.pdf)

Those notices and the final answer keys remain in the
[NTA notice archive](https://www.nta.ac.in/NoticeBoardArchive), but no stable,
complete, direct official English Paper 1 PDFs for the 2021 shifts were found.
One live NTA `ExamPaper` candidate discovered during the audit was inspected
and excluded because its PDF header identifies it as the 29 June 2022 second
shift in Odia, not a 2021 English paper.

No coaching-site reconstruction, memory-based paper or candidate response
sheet was substituted. A future production package should be prepared only
after complete official copies become available or the user supplies
redistribution-approved originals.
