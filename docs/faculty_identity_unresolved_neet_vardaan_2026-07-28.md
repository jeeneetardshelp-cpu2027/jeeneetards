# Unresolved NEET Vardaan faculty identities — 28 July 2026

## Decision

Courses 118 and 119 remain unnormalized. This is an evidence boundary, not a
schema or tooling problem.

The official Competition Wallah playlists establish the short per-video
credits, but their public titles and descriptions do not publish full teacher
names. Current official PW catalogues contain plausible full-name candidates,
but do not connect those identities to these exact historical videos.

No SQL package is prepared for either course.

## Evidence required to reopen

At least one first-party source must connect each short credit to the exact
Vardaan playlist or video, such as:

- an official description naming the teacher;
- an official PW page embedding or linking the exact video under the full name;
- a reliable self-identification in the lecture transcript;
- preserved original PW metadata that contains both the video ID and full name.

Until then:

- keep the truthful combined legacy labels;
- do not create normalized teachers or aliases for these courses;
- do not infer from face, voice, subject, roster membership, or likely cohort;
- do not partially normalize course 119 merely because Pankaj Sijariya is
  independently known.
